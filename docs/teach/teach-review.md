- `cards-hand` (19) — a fan of five cards

- `dice-cup` (3) — a cup with three dice spilling out
- `map-armies` (8) — a map of four regions with meeples in three
- `map-board` (4) — a board with routes/regions and a few pieces
- `workers-board` (2) — a board with action spots, meeples on two
