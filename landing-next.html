Get your shelf

From the shelf to the table in four steps

The same loop every game night, minus the twenty minutes of "I don't mind, you choose."

ShelfPickVotePlay
  1. 1 Shelf

    Your collection becomes a page

    Enter your BoardGameGeek username and your games arrive with covers, player counts and play times. The shelf gets its own link, so anyone can browse it from a phone.

  2. 2 Pick

    Shortlist the contenders

    Start a game night and tap the games worth arguing about. Filter by vibe, players and time first, or let Tonight's surprise deal a shortlist for you.

  3. 3 Vote

    Everyone votes from their own phone

    Friends join with a link, a QR code or a short code. No account, just a name. Each person swipes right to play, left to pass, and nobody sees anyone else's vote.

  4. 4 Play

    The votes are counted, one game wins

    The table watches the count together and one game takes it. If two tie, a rematch votes on just those two. Then get the box down.
