BoardgameShelf

Privacy policy

Last updated 8 September 2026

BoardgameShelf is a free, non-commercial hobby site run by one person. It shows a board game collection as a browsable shelf and lets friends vote on what to play. This page explains what the site stores and why.

What is collected

  • Sign-in details. When you sign in with Google or an email link, the site receives your email address and, from Google, your name and profile picture URL. These identify your account. The site never sees your Google password.
  • Your BoardGameGeek username and the list of games you have marked as owned on BoardGameGeek, together with your rating, play count and comment for each. This is fetched from BoardGameGeek's public API only when you press Sync, and only for the username you entered.
  • Game night activity. When you host or join a game night, the site stores the name you type, the games in the deck, and your play or pass votes so the table can see a result. No account is needed to join.
  • Basic visit statistics from Vercel Web Analytics: page views and general device and country information. This is aggregated and does not identify you.

What is public

Your shelf at /u/<your BGG username> is public by default so you can share it. It shows your BGG username, the display name you chose, and the games you own with their covers and details. Your email address, ratings, play counts and comments are not shown on the shelf. You can make your shelf private in Settings; then only you can see it while signed in.

Where it is stored

Account, profile, collection and game night data are stored in a Supabase database. The site itself is hosted on Vercel. Game covers are not downloaded; they are loaded in your browser directly from BoardGameGeek's image servers, which means BoardGameGeek may see the request as it would for any image on their own site.

What it is used for

Only to run the site: signing you in, showing your shelf, syncing your collection, and running game nights. The data is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for anything else. There are no ads and no tracking cookies. The site keeps a sign-in session in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in.

Third parties

  • Google, if you sign in with Google.
  • Supabase, for sign-in and the database.
  • Vercel, for hosting and analytics.
  • BoardGameGeek, for game data and cover images. BoardgameShelf is not affiliated with or endorsed by BoardGameGeek.

Deleting your data

Open Settings and choose Delete account. This removes your profile, your synced collection and your sign-in straight away. Votes and names from game nights you hosted stay attached to those game nights so the results still make sense, but are no longer linked to you. If you can no longer sign in, email nok.naruephon@gmail.com from the address you used and it will be done by hand. Game details shared across users (the cache of names, covers and stats fetched from BoardGameGeek) are not personal data and stay.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it.
